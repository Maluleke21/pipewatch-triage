PipeWatch Triage Demo

Welcome to the Mnwanati Solutions hackathon prototype. This platform monitors simulated Marshalltown night-flow water telemetry to detect silent underground pipe bursts.

  • Live Telemetry: The Python backend is simulating 1:00 AM - 4:00 AM flow data.
  • AI Detection: A Scikit-learn Isolation Forest model identifies critical pressure drops.
  • Automated Dispatch: Red pins drop on the map in real-time when intervention is required.
